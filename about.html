About

I'm Prateek, and I'm learning digital marketing by doing it.

Who I am

Marketon is my personal brand and portfolio. It is not an agency, and I don't present myself as one. It is the place where I show what I'm learning and the practical work I do to apply it.

I'm interested in how businesses get found, understood and chosen online, and I want to build real, useful skills in that space.

My learning journey

I'm at the beginning of my digital marketing career. Right now I'm building a broad foundation across SEO, content marketing, social media and analytics, rather than specializing too early.

My plan is to learn the fundamentals, work on real projects, and then choose a specialization based on what I find I'm good at and what the work shows me. SEO is the area I'm starting with.

My approach

Learning, experimenting and practical execution.

  • Learning

    I start with the fundamentals and make sure I understand why something works before I use it.

  • Experimenting

    I test ideas on small projects, so I can see what happens and learn from it.

  • Practical execution

    I turn what I learn into finished work, and I describe that work honestly, including what it is and what it isn't.
